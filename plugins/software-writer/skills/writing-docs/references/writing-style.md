# Writing Style

## Headings predict their content

A heading names what its section holds, because other surfaces cite it as `§<heading name>` and a vague heading breaks those cross-refs.

## Jargon discipline

Project-specific jargon is defined once at the surface `docs.jargon_home` names and never re-defined; point at the definition instead.

Stack and standard-library vocabulary stays undefined.

## Diagrams earn their place

Cross-module flow and containment relationships qualify for a diagram. Do not strip an existing earning diagram during a single-owner pass.
