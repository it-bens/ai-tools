# Planner-invented phrasings

Phrasings that signal a planner-invented decision and fail the citable-source test under Step 4 §Scope and guardrail skepticism unless the plan attaches a source. Acceptable sources: a spec line naming the user's choice; project posture documented in `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, or equivalent project surfaces; a prior commit; an active named follow-up.

- "X plan adds Y on top later", or any forward reference to a plan that may not exist yet.
- "intentionally out of scope" without naming the chooser.
- "to keep this contained" / "to keep this focused" / "to keep the diff small" (smallness as virtue).
- "the simpler approach" / "the cleaner approach" (simplicity as authority).
- "Promote / Introduce / Replace ... so that ..." where the rationale is a planner judgment rather than a cited source.
- "DESIGN-NNN" or similar plan-internal rule citation where the rule itself is not user-attributed.
- "deliberate", "intentional", or "by design" labels without user attribution and date.
