The report export is slow because every row triggers its own lookup. I see three ways to fix it:

A. Batch the lookups per page of 500 rows. Smallest change, roughly 4x faster.
B. Precompute the lookup table once per export. About 10x faster, but it holds the table in memory.
C. Move the export to a nightly job and serve the stored file. Fastest for readers, but the data is up to a day old.

Which option do you prefer?
